# WorldBrief

**Understand the world. Find the sources. Learn beyond the headlines.**

**理解世界，追溯信息源，超越新闻标题。**

WorldBrief is an open-source project designed to help ordinary people understand the world with less information noise and more context.

Every day, we encounter countless short videos, headlines, social media posts, and opinions. But more information does not necessarily mean better understanding. Important facts are often buried in endless feeds, original sources are difficult to find, and unfamiliar topics can be hard to understand without the right background knowledge.

WorldBrief aims to make information easier to discover, verify, and understand.

Our goal is simple: **help people spend less time scrolling and more time understanding.**

WorldBrief 是一个面向普通人的开源项目，旨在减少信息噪声，帮助人们更轻松地获取信息、理解世界。

每天，我们都会接触大量短视频、新闻标题、社交媒体内容和各种观点。但信息更多，并不意味着理解更深入。重要事实可能被淹没在无尽的信息流中，原始来源难以查找，陌生领域也常常因为缺乏背景知识而难以理解。

WorldBrief 希望让信息更容易被发现、核实和理解。

我们的目标很简单：**减少无意义的信息刷取，把更多时间用于真正理解世界。**

> **Project Status / 项目状态**
>
> This project is currently in the planning and research stage. Proposed features are not yet implemented.
>
> 本项目目前处于规划与研究阶段，文中提及的功能均为规划目标，尚不代表已经实现。

## Why WorldBrief? / 为什么需要 WorldBrief？

We focus on five common problems.

我们希望解决普通人在信息获取过程中经常遇到的五个问题。

### 1. Too Much Information, Too Little Direction
### 信息很多，却不知道应该关注什么

Help people discover important developments without endlessly scrolling through feeds.

帮助用户发现值得关注的事件，而不是每天无休止地刷信息流。

### 2. Too Much Content, Too Little Time
### 内容太多，没有时间全部看完

Explain complex events in concise, consistent, and accessible language.

用简洁、统一、易理解的方式解释复杂事件，降低获取信息的时间成本。

### 3. Too Much Promotion, Too Little Transparency
### 宣传信息太多，信息来源不够透明

Prioritize original documents and clearly identified sources over unsupported claims and promotional commentary.

优先呈现原始文件和清晰标注的信息来源，减少对缺乏依据的说法和宣传性解读的依赖。

### 4. Too Much Curiosity, Too Few Starting Points
### 想深入了解，却不知道从哪里开始

Help people explore unfamiliar subjects step by step, starting with the basics.

帮助用户从基础概念入手，逐步理解陌生领域，而不是一开始就面对大量专业术语。

### 5. Too Many Claims, Too Few Direct Sources
### 各种说法很多，却找不到原始依据

Make it easier to access official websites, original reports, public datasets, and primary materials.

帮助用户直接找到官方网站、原始报告、公开数据和其他一手资料。

## Core Features / 核心功能

### 1. Daily Briefing / 每日信息简报

**Discover what matters without endless scrolling.**

**不必无休止刷信息，也能了解值得关注的事情。**

The planned daily briefing will aim to:

- Highlight noteworthy developments from around the world.
- Explain what happened in plain language.
- Provide background and explain why an event matters.
- Identify publication dates and information sources.
- Link to original materials whenever available.

计划中的每日简报将致力于：

- 筛选全球范围内值得关注的事件。
- 用普通人容易理解的语言解释事件。
- 补充必要背景，说明事件为什么值得关注。
- 标注发布时间和信息来源。
- 在可获取的情况下，直接链接原始资料。

The goal is not to publish as much as possible, but to help readers identify what deserves their attention.

我们的目标不是尽可能多地发布信息，而是帮助用户判断哪些信息值得关注。

### 2. Official Sources & Primary Information / 官方来源与原始信息

**Go directly to the source.**

**不止阅读二手解读，尽可能直达信息源。**

WorldBrief aims to organize links to:

- Government departments and public institutions.
- International organizations.
- Official company announcements and reports.
- Universities and research institutions.
- Public datasets, research papers, and original documents.

WorldBrief 计划整理以下类型的信息来源：

- 政府部门和公共机构。
- 国际组织。
- 企业官方公告和报告。
- 大学与研究机构。
- 公开数据集、研究论文和原始文件。

Official sources can provide valuable first-hand information, but they do not automatically represent the complete picture. Where appropriate, WorldBrief will distinguish official statements from independently verified facts, analysis, and unresolved questions.

官方来源可以提供重要的一手信息，但不一定代表事件的完整面貌。在适当情况下，WorldBrief 将区分官方表述、经过独立核实的事实、分析观点和仍待确认的问题。

### 3. AI-Guided Learning / AI 引导式学习

**Turn curiosity into understanding.**

**让一时的好奇变成真正的理解。**

The planned learning experience will help users:

- Start with essential concepts and background knowledge.
- Understand unfamiliar terms and institutions.
- Follow a structured learning path.
- Find original documents and authoritative references.
- Ask follow-up questions and explore further.

计划中的 AI 学习功能将帮助用户：

- 从必要的基础概念和背景知识开始。
- 理解陌生的术语、组织和机构。
- 按照结构化的学习路径逐步深入。
- 查找原始文件和权威参考资料。
- 继续提问，并自主探索相关主题。

AI should help people understand information, not replace original sources or discourage independent thinking.

AI 应当帮助用户理解信息，而不是取代原始来源，更不应削弱用户独立思考和查证的能力。

### 4. Topic Exploration / 主题探索

Important events often connect to history, science, technology, economics, public policy, and other fields.

WorldBrief aims to connect daily information with longer-term learning resources, helping users move from a single question to a deeper understanding.

重要事件往往与历史、科学、技术、经济、公共政策等领域相互关联。

WorldBrief 希望将每日信息与长期学习资料连接起来，帮助用户从一个问题出发，逐步建立对相关领域的系统认识。

## How It Works / 使用流程

The intended experience follows five steps.

我们希望用户能够按照以下五个步骤使用 WorldBrief。

1. **Discover / 发现：** Find a noteworthy event or topic in the daily briefing. 在每日简报中发现值得关注的事件或主题。
2. **Understand / 理解：** Read a concise explanation and relevant background. 阅读简明解释和相关背景。
3. **Verify / 核实：** Open the original source and examine the available evidence. 进入原始来源，查看相关证据。
4. **Explore / 探索：** Follow a structured learning path with AI assistance. 在 AI 的辅助下，按照学习路径逐步深入。
5. **Learn / 学习：** Continue through official documents, public data, and reliable references. 通过官方文件、公开数据和可靠资料继续学习。

**Discover → Understand → Verify → Explore → Learn**

**发现信息 → 理解背景 → 查证来源 → 深入探索 → 持续学习**

## Design Principles / 设计原则

- **Clarity over quantity / 重质量，不堆数量：** Prefer useful information over endless content. 优先提供有价值的信息，而不是无限增加内容。
- **Sources over slogans / 来源优先于口号：** Make original materials easy to access. 让原始资料更容易被找到。
- **Context over isolated headlines / 背景优先于孤立标题：** Explain relevant background and connections. 解释事件的背景及其关联。
- **Transparency over false certainty / 透明胜于虚假的确定性：** Identify sources, distinguish facts from interpretations, and acknowledge uncertainty. 标明来源，区分事实与解释，并承认不确定性。
- **Learning over dependency / 鼓励学习，而非依赖：** Use AI to develop understanding and independent research skills. 利用 AI 培养理解能力和独立研究能力。
- **Global perspective / 全球视野：** Explore information across countries, languages, institutions, and viewpoints. 关注不同国家、语言、机构和观点的信息。
- **Accessible to everyone / 面向普通人：** Explain complex topics in language that beginners can understand. 用零基础用户也能理解的语言解释复杂主题。
- **Responsible information use / 负责任地使用信息：** Respect copyright, attribution, access restrictions, and applicable licenses. 尊重版权、署名要求、访问限制和相关许可。

## Roadmap / 项目路线图

WorldBrief will develop gradually, beginning with research and documentation rather than complex software development.

WorldBrief 将从研究和文档整理开始，逐步推进，而不是一开始就进行复杂的软件开发。

- [ ] Document the problems the project aims to solve. 记录项目要解决的问题。
- [ ] Research existing open-source projects. 调研现有开源项目。
- [ ] Identify potential areas of differentiation. 确定可能的差异化方向。

- [ ] Define source evaluation and verification guidelines. 制定来源评估与核查规则。
- [ ] Design a consistent daily briefing template. 设计统一的每日简报模板。
- [ ] Design a beginner-friendly learning path template. 设计适合初学者的学习路径模板。

- [ ] Create sample daily briefings. 制作示例简报。

- [ ] Check whether original sources are easy to find. 检查原始来源是否容易查找。
- [ ] Evaluate the usefulness of learning paths. 评估学习路径是否实用。

### Phase 4 — Build a Minimal Usable Version / 第四阶段：建立最小可用版本

- [ ] Determine a suitable publishing format. 确定合适的内容发布形式。
- [ ] Explore RSS feeds and other permitted information sources. 探索 RSS 和其他允许使用的信息源。
- [ ] Evaluate AI tools for summarization and guided learning. 评估用于摘要和引导学习的 AI 工具。
- [ ] Publish an initial version for real-world use. 发布可供实际使用的初始版本。
- [ ] Improve the product based on feedback. 根据反馈改进产品。

### Phase 5 — Expand Carefully / 第五阶段：逐步扩展

- [ ] Explore personalization and topic subscriptions. 探索个性化推荐和主题订阅。
- [ ] Improve source comparison and event tracking. 改进来源对比和事件追踪。
- [ ] Develop searchable topic archives. 建立可搜索的主题档案。
- [ ] Introduce automation where it improves quality and reliability. 在有助于提高质量和可靠性的环节引入自动化。
- [ ] Invite community contributions. 邀请社区参与贡献。

*The roadmap is provisional and may change as research and testing reveal what users need.*

*本路线图为初步规划，将根据研究结果和实际用户需求进行调整。*

## What WorldBrief Is Not / WorldBrief 不是什么

WorldBrief is not intended to be:

- Another platform designed to maximize scrolling time.
- A replacement for original documents or professional journalism.
- A platform that treats every official statement as automatically true.
- An AI system that presents unverified claims as established facts.
- A content aggregator that republishes material without respecting source rights and licenses.

WorldBrief 不打算成为：

- 另一个以延长用户刷信息时间为目标的平台。
- 原始文件或专业新闻报道的替代品。
- 将所有官方表述自动视为事实的平台。
- 把未经核实的说法当作确定事实的 AI 系统。
- 不尊重来源权益和内容许可、随意转载材料的信息聚合器。

## Who Is This For? / 适合哪些人？

WorldBrief is intended for anyone who wants to understand the world better, including people who:

- Want to follow important global developments but have limited time.
- Prefer concise explanations to long videos and commentary.
- Want to distinguish original information from second-hand interpretations.
- Are curious about unfamiliar topics but do not know where to start.
- Want to use AI as a learning assistant rather than simply consume more content.

WorldBrief 面向所有希望更好地理解世界的人，包括：

- 想了解全球重要动态，但时间有限的人。
- 更喜欢简洁解释，而不是长视频和冗长评论的人。
- 希望区分原始信息与二手解读的人。
- 对陌生领域感兴趣，却不知道从哪里入门的人。
- 希望将 AI 用作学习助手，而不是单纯消费更多内容的人。

No specialized knowledge should be required to get started.

使用 WorldBrief 不应要求用户具备专业知识。

我们希望未来每个人都能在不被信息淹没的情况下获取重要信息，能够追溯信息来源，并有信心地探索陌生领域。
