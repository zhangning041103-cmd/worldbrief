# WorldBrief — Vision

# 中文版

## 1. 项目愿景

WorldBrief 希望帮助普通人更轻松地获取信息、理解世界，并培养独立查证和持续学习的能力。

我们生活在一个信息丰富但注意力稀缺的时代。短视频、社交媒体、新闻和各种观点不断涌入，人们却不一定能够接触到原始信息，也不一定有足够的背景知识理解复杂事件。

WorldBrief 希望让信息获取从被动浏览转向主动理解。

## 2. 我们要解决的问题

- 信息数量庞大，重要内容容易被淹没。
- 二手解读和宣传内容很多，原始来源不容易找到。
- 不同网站的信息分散，查找和核实需要花费大量时间。
- 普通人面对陌生领域时，往往不知道从哪里开始学习。
- AI 可以生成解释，但用户仍需要了解信息来源并独立判断。

## 3. 我们服务的人群

WorldBrief 面向所有希望了解世界的普通人，不限定年龄、职业或专业领域。

我们尤其关注那些希望了解全球动态、减少无效信息消费、直接查找原始资料，并通过 AI 学习新知识的人。

## 4. 核心方向

### 每日信息

用简洁、易懂的方式介绍值得关注的全球事件，并提供必要背景。

### 官方来源

帮助用户找到政府、国际组织、大学、研究机构、企业等发布的官方信息和原始资料。

### AI 引导学习

帮助用户从基础概念开始理解陌生主题，并逐步进入更深入的资料和研究。

### 主题探索

把每日事件与相关背景知识、关键概念和学习资源连接起来。

## 5. 核心原则

- 来源透明，尽可能提供原始链接。
- 区分事实、官方表述、分析和推测。
- 承认不确定性，不把未经核实的内容当作事实。
- 优先考虑信息质量，而不是内容数量。
- 使用 AI 帮助理解，而不是代替独立思考。
- 尊重版权、来源署名和内容使用许可。
- 让没有专业背景的人也能理解复杂信息。

## 6. 长期目标

逐步建立一个整合每日信息、官方来源导航和 AI 引导学习的开放平台。

用户可以从一条信息开始，了解事件背景，找到原始依据，再进一步学习相关领域。

## 7. 当前阶段

目前项目处于规划与研究阶段。

现阶段优先完成产品定位、同类项目研究、信息源整理和内容模板设计。待这些基础经过验证后，再决定如何开发最小可用版本。

# English Version

## 1. Vision

WorldBrief aims to help ordinary people access information, understand the world, and develop independent research and lifelong learning skills.

We live in an age of abundant information but limited attention. Short videos, social media, news, and opinions constantly compete for attention, while original sources and the background needed to understand complex events can be difficult to find.

WorldBrief aims to shift information consumption from passive scrolling to active understanding.

## 2. Problems We Want to Address

- Important information is easily buried in overwhelming content.
- Commentary and promotional material can make original sources difficult to identify.
- Information is scattered across websites, making research and verification time-consuming.
- Beginners often do not know where to start when exploring unfamiliar subjects.
- AI can generate explanations, but users still need traceable sources and independent judgment.

## 3. Who We Serve

WorldBrief is for anyone who wants to understand the world, regardless of age, profession, or area of expertise.

We particularly aim to support people who want to follow global developments, reduce unproductive information consumption, access original materials, and use AI to learn new subjects.

## 4. Core Directions

### Daily Briefing

Explain noteworthy global developments in clear, accessible language and provide relevant background.

### Official Sources

Help users find official information and primary materials from governments, international organizations, universities, research institutions, and companies.

### AI-Guided Learning

Help users understand unfamiliar subjects from the basics and gradually explore more advanced materials.

### Topic Exploration

Connect daily events with background knowledge, key concepts, and learning resources.

## 5. Core Principles

- Transparency and traceable sources.
- Clear distinctions between facts, official statements, analysis, and speculation.
- Honest communication of uncertainty.
- Information quality over quantity.
- AI as a tool for understanding, not a replacement for independent thinking.
- Respect for copyright, attribution, and content licenses.
- Accessibility for people without specialized knowledge.

## 6. Long-Term Goal

Gradually build an open platform that integrates daily information, official-source navigation, and AI-guided learning.

Users should be able to start with a single piece of information, understand its context, find the original evidence, and continue learning about the subject.

## 7. Current Stage

WorldBrief is currently in the planning and research stage.

The immediate priorities are to clarify the product vision, study related projects, organize information sources, and design content templates. A minimal usable version will be considered after these foundations have been evaluated.
